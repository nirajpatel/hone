# Beans vs. Bag: Terminology Guide

This doc captures how we use **beans** and **bag** in the product so copy stays consistent and clear for users and for us.

---

## The split

| Term | Meaning | When to use |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Beans** | The coffee *product* or your *collection* of coffees (roaster + name, the “what”). | Page/section name, selector label, empty states (“no beans yet”), search/headers (“Recently used beans”, “Search beans”), and any time we mean “which coffee” or “coffee in the app.” |
| **Bag** | One *physical bag* (a single purchase/roast/entry). | Add/Save/Edit Bag, New Bag, Change Bag, Add New Bag, Add Another Bag, “this bag,” “previous bag,” bag photos, bag status, timeline “New Bag” divider, and any time we mean “this one bag” or “which bag.” |

**Short version:**  
**Beans** = what coffee / your list.  
**Bag** = one physical bag and actions on it.

---

## Why this split

- **Beans** matches how people talk about coffee as a product: “which beans,” “my beans,” “no beans yet.”
- **Bag** matches the real-world unit: one bag you open, brew from, and eventually finish.
- In the new-brew flow, users first choose **beans** (which coffee), then may switch **bag** (e.g. same beans, different roast date) via “Change Bag” / “Add New Bag.” So the selector is “Beans”; the sub-actions are “Bag.”

---

## Guidelines for future copy

1. **Choosing or listing coffee?** → Use **beans** (e.g. “which beans,” “no beans yet,” “add your beans”).
2. **Adding/editing one entry, or switching which bag you’re brewing?** → Use **bag** (e.g. “Add Bag,” “this bag,” “Change Bag,” “New Bag”).
3. **New UI that’s about the physical package or one purchase?** → Prefer **bag**.
4. **New UI that’s about the product or your collection?** → Prefer **beans**.
5. **Unclear?** Ask: “Are we talking about *which coffee* or *which physical bag*?” → beans vs bag.

---

## Edge cases we decided

- **Beans page “Add” button:** We use “Add Bag” (one bag at a time), not “Add Beans,” so the CTA matches the unit. The page name stays “Beans.”
- **Empty state body copy:** We use “beans” when describing what’s missing (“no beans yet,” “add your beans,” “adding your beans and equipment”) and “Add Bag” for the button.
- **“Photos are saved with your beans”:** We use “Photos are saved with **this bag**” when the context is a single bag (e.g. add/edit flow), because the form is for that one bag.
- **QR / legal copy:** “track beans,” “coffee bag” (photos) are fine as-is; they fit the product vs. physical-bag meaning above.
- **“Bean Temp” / “Bean Temperature”:** Left as-is—industry term for brew/coffee temperature, not our beans vs. bag product model.
